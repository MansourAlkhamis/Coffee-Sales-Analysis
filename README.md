# Coffee-Sales-Analysis
nalyzed 3,547 coffee transactions from a single café covering a full year (March 2024 – March 2025). The analysis explores revenue trends, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns across different times of day.

## Overview
Analysis of 3,547 coffee transactions from a single café covering 
a full year (March 2024 – March 2025). Built using Power BI.

## Key Findings
- Latte and Americano with Milk together drive 44% of total revenue
- March is the peak revenue month generating nearly 15K — highest 
  single month in the year
- Sales are consistent across morning, afternoon, and night — 
  unusual for a café which typically peaks in the morning
- 100% cashless business — all 3,547 transactions were card payments 
  with zero cash
- Total annual revenue: 112K

## Tools Used
Power BI, Excel

## Data Source
Coffee shop transaction records — 3,547 rows, 12 months
